NSW Year 8 Geography • Water in the world

Using maps to understand water in the world

This quiz focuses on using geographical tools to explore global and Australian patterns in water distribution, quantity and quality. It uses sourced maps and classroom-built graphics to help students read cartographic conventions, identify relationships between physical and human features, and interpret spatial patterns.

Task features

  • 10 questions using global and Australian water-related maps.
  • Emphasis on cartographic conventions and spatial patterns.
  • Multiple choice and short-response items in a full-screen layout.
  • Automatic marking for multiple choice items and exportable results.
